Trade · Bridge

Exchange and wrap Bitcoin.

Trade is the non-custodial UI for confidential assets and BTCX. Bridge pegs BTC to BTCX 1:1. Prices come from the Oracle. Docs: docs.privacydatacoin.com.

Trade

PDC Trade

Order book and Easy Swap at trade.privacydatacoin.com. Sign-in is PDC Companion against your own wallet RPC. Matched trades settle as ionic swaps on-chain — the website does not take custody.

Bridge

BTC ↔ BTCX

Deposit BTC with the invoice OP_RETURN to mint BTCX (confidential asset, 8 decimals). Burn BTCX to withdraw BTC. Peg is 1 sat → 1 sat. Live mins, withdraw fee, confirmations, and pause flags are on GET /api/bridge/status.

PDC Oracle

Signed USD feed that Trade verifies. Dedicated page covers the API, sources, and Ed25519 envelope.

Swaps

Ionic swaps

Atomic exchange attachments in the wallet path. Trade builds on generate_ionic_swap_proposal / accept. Read the proposal before you accept — accepting spends what the hex says it spends.
