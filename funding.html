PDC Funding

Fund the work that moves PDC forward.

Community proposals with transparent escrow and milestone payouts. Donate to an integrated address; admins release funds when milestones land. The live board lives on the Funding site — this page is the overview and the link into it.

Open Funding funding.privacydatacoin.com

01 · Propose

Community proposals

Work that matters to PDC — docs, tooling, integrations, research — can be listed as a funded proposal with a clear target in PDC.

02 · Escrow

Transparent escrow

Donations go to an integrated address tied to the proposal. Progress against the target is visible on the Funding board.

03 · Payout

Milestone releases

When agreed milestones land, admins release escrow. Payouts follow the work, not an open-ended tip jar.

Where to go

Use the Funding site for live proposals.

Browse open and completed proposals, connect a wallet when you want to donate, and track how much remains against each target.

Scope

What this is not

Funding is separate from Trade, the Oracle feed, and wallet custody. It does not sell PDC and it is not an investment product — it routes voluntary support to listed work.
