Privacy Data Coin

Privacy across the project

How Privacy Data Coin treats privacy on the chain, in the software we publish, and on the public surfaces we run. Effective 27 October 2026.

On the chain

PDC is a confidential ledger. A normal wallet transfer hides the sender, the receiver, the amount, and the asset type. Stealth addresses, d/v-CLSAG, commitments, confidential assets, and Zarcanum staking are part of the protocol, not optional modes. Observers can verify proofs; they cannot read those fields from the public chain.

What we do not collect

The project does not run a user account system for the coin, the desktop wallet, simplewallet, or Trade. We do not sell personal data, do not run advertising SDKs on our sites, and do not ask for KYC to download binaries or sync a node. Seeds, private keys, and wallet files stay under your control.

Wallets and nodes

GUI, CLI, and mobile clients store the wallet file, seed, keys, and history on the device you choose. They talk to a PDC node you select (or run yourself). A remote node can see your IP, the blocks you request, and transactions you broadcast. Your view key is not sent for a normal scan. Anyone can run a full node; you are not required to use a project-operated endpoint.

Trade and Oracle

Trade is a non-custodial UI. It does not hold your funds or create an account profile. The Oracle publishes a signed USD price feed and related public API responses. Those services may keep ordinary server logs (IP, path, time) for security and reliability. They are not used to build an advertising profile.

Website, docs, explorer

privacydatacoin.com, docs.privacydatacoin.com, and explorer.privacydatacoin.com are public, mostly static or read-only surfaces. They do not set account cookies and do not run third-party ads. Hosts may keep standard access logs. Explorer data is the public chain view, subject to the protocol’s privacy rules above.

Public channels

Telegram, Medium, GitHub, and email are how the project communicates. Those platforms have their own policies. Messages you send to privacydatacoin@proton.me are used to answer you, not for marketing lists.

Mobile wallet note

Where a PDC Wallet mobile app is published, wallet material stays on the device. Optional biometric unlock uses the platform keychain. Camera and photo access are only for QR or backup flows you start. The same remote-node limits as above apply.
